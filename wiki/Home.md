# FAST-OAD-CS23-HE

Welcome to the FAST-OAD-CS23-HE wiki! This wiki is designed to provide comprehensive detailed descriptions of the 
FAST-OAD-CS23-HE to support future developments.

For user, please check the [feature documentation](https://fast-oad-cs23-he.readthedocs.io/) that explains the 
mechanism of the FAST-OAD-CS23-HE and how to use it.

For developers or researchers, please check [model description](../models/model_index) for detailed 
descriptions of the OpenMDAO disciplines, [Powertrain builder description](../pt_builder/pt_builder_index) for detailed 
powertrain builder mechanism description, and [GUI description](../gui/gui_index) for detailed descriptions of the 
pre-processing and post-processing services.

To install the FAST-OAD-CS23-HE or other FAST-OAD projects, please check the 
[installation guide](https://github.com/fast-aircraft-design/FAST-OAD/wiki/Installing-and-using-Poetry)
